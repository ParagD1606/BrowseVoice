Good morning everyone. Let's start with a simple problem.

Today, most websites are designed primarily in English. But a large number of people in India are more comfortable communicating in their regional languages. Because of this language barrier, they often struggle to navigate websites, understand information, fill out online forms, and access essential digital services.

Even for someone who understands English, navigating complicated websites and completing lengthy forms can be frustrating.

That is the problem we are trying to solve with Browse Voice.

Browse Voice is a multilingual, AI-powered Chrome extension that lets people control websites using natural voice commands in Indian languages. You can speak or type. It guides you one step at a time, while you stay in control.

For example, a user can simply say, "Mera electricity bill check karo," and the agent can navigate the website, enter the required details, and retrieve the bill.

We use Gnani AI for speech recognition and text-to-speech, and Gemini or other LLMs to understand instructions and plan browser actions. You can choose Gemini, Hugging Face, an OpenAI-compatible model, or an offline mock planner for demos.

Our agent observes the webpage, plans actions, validates them for safety, and executes them. It can click buttons, fill forms, select dropdowns, scroll, navigate, ask follow-up questions, and complete multi-step workflows. Hindi answers can be converted to English text for supported form fields.

Live execution steps appear in the activity panel, so every click and fill is visible.

We have strong safety features. Passwords, OTPs, and payment card details stay user-only. Submits and payments require explicit confirmation. And a global Stop control can abort every running task instantly.

Browse Voice also supports visual webpage understanding. Select an area on the page, ask a question about that region, and get an answer grounded in what you highlighted.

It can index a website and answer questions with site knowledge search, powered by the knowledge backend.

And before you trust a page, website risk assessment scores safety signals and explains caution reasons.

Need more than one thing done? Browse Voice can split a command into up to four independent browser tasks that run in parallel, each with its own tab, planner loop, and activity card.

The interface itself speaks your language too: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Malayalam, Indian English, and auto-detect Indic.

Our vision is simple: making the internet accessible to everyone, regardless of their language or technical expertise.

Browse Voice. Your voice. Your language. Your web.
